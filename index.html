Choose an IRA that fits your retirement strategy

Different IRA structures can provide different tax treatment and contribution considerations. Eligibility and rules vary by individual circumstances.

T

Traditional IRA

Contributions may receive tax advantages depending on eligibility, while withdrawals are generally taxed as ordinary income under applicable rules.

R

Roth IRA

Contributions are generally made with after-tax dollars. Qualified distributions may be tax-free subject to applicable requirements.

S

Simplified IRA

Retirement arrangements designed for qualifying small-business owners and employees, subject to applicable eligibility and contribution rules.
